# Mauka Wear And Remake

Mauka. — Make secondhand second nature.

Mauka means chance in Hindi. It’s a secondhand fashion marketplace for shoppers in India, featuring both Western and Indian clothing and circular-fashion services.


Fashion Deserves a Second Chance
MAUKA is a sustainable fashion e-commerce platform designed to promote circular fashion, reduce textile waste and make sustainable clothing accessible and affordable.


About the Project
MAUKA goes beyond traditional e-commerce by bringing clothing resale, exchange, styling, upcycling and customized tailoring together on a single platform.

Five Pillars of MAUKA
Feature	Description
RESELL	Buy and sell pre-loved clothing.
RE:EXCHANGE	Exchange clothing without monetary transactions.
RE:IMAGINE	Discover new ways to style existing garments.
RE:CREATE	Transform old garments into upcycled products for resale.
RE:SOURCE	Connect with tailors to transform unused fabrics into customized outfits.


Key Features
- Product catalogue and category-based navigation
- Product search
- Product listings and image uploads
- Shopping cart and checkout
- User authentication and access control
- Form validation and session management
- Order management
Objectives
- Reduce textile waste.
- Promote circular fashion and responsible consumption.
- Make sustainable fashion affordable.
- Support local tailors and independent creators.
